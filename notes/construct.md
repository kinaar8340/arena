# Minimal construct ontology

Dictionary for loading language. Not forced by the math. Not a fifth through ninth operational word in the companion note.

The note still defines four words as maps of \(p\): life, catalog, correction, alignment (`tex/words.tex`). Everything below is a reading of those four, plus the Hopf remainder, plus the gate \(\sigma(Z)\).

Claim label: **Model** (dictionary). The incompleteness sentence is the only part that is forced if you stay inside the attached Model: a complete catalog of loaded objects still cannot invert the fiber.

## Five primitives, each operational

Keep each one a space, a map, or a functional of a density \(p\). White empty space is undifferentiated potential; load only these.

### 1. Arena (construct / remainder)

Empty of named objects. Hopf picture: the fiber \(S^{1}\) plus the fact that the base cannot invert it. Not “nothing”: the space in which loading is possible.

Not a term in \(Q\). It is the configuration manifold (generic \(\Psi\), or \(S^{3}\)) before a catalog item is instantiated.

### 2. Loader (life / helicoid / \(\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{O}}\))

Continuous transport. Residual self-image, flow, the thing that can still move when no catalog item has been instantiated.

\[
\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{O}}p=-\nabla\cdot(O[\Psi]\,p)
\quad\text{or}\quad
-\operatorname{div}_{S^{3}}(\mathrm{X}_{\mathrm{O}}\,p).
\]

\(\sigma=0\) is pure life. On the Hopf stage, \(\mathrm{X}_{\mathrm{O}}(q)=iq\) is the right-invariant generator of left common-phase on QGA’s \(h\) (`notes/hopf.md`). The left-invariant field \(q\mapsto qi\) is not used.

### 3. Loaded scene (catalog / catenoid / \(\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{C}}\))

Named, jumpable, projectable things: lattice sites, observables that factor through \(h:S^{3}\to S^{2}\). A flux lattice already was a catalog. The new move is admitting that the catalog is gated, not identical to the arena.

\[
\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{C}}p
=\int\bigl[w(\Psi\mid\Psi')p(\Psi')-w(\Psi'\mid\Psi)p(\Psi)\bigr]\,d\Psi'.
\]

Gain minus loss is already inside. No extra minus on this lobe.

### 4. Gate \(\sigma(Z)\)

How much of the arena is allowed to freeze into named objects. \(Z\) is coarse: no equation for \(Z\) in this note. Protocol S1 is a sweep of the gate with no closed-loop correction: freeze the vector field and the kernel, vary \(\sigma\), watch when jumps dominate transport.

“Without correction, adaptation takes control” is that report, not a motto.

### 5. Lens

Only the motion of the density is seen:

\[
\frac{\partial p}{\partial t}=\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{O}}p+\sigma(Z)\,\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{C}}p.
\]

There is no third operator. Past / present / future in a time triangle is the time parameter of \(\partial p/\partial t\), not a separate substance.

Correction \(C:(p,e)\mapsto(\sigma',w')\) with \(e=A[p]-A^{\ast}\) is defined in the note and unused in S1. Alignment is a named \(A[p]\). Life-drift \(D(\sigma,t)=\mathrm{KL}(p_{t}^{\sigma}\Vert p_{t}^{0})\) is not alignment.

## What a basic system has to do

A model theory here is not a list of symbols. It is a closed Markov generator plus a named alignment functional

\[
\frac{\partial p}{\partial t}=\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{O}}p+\sigma(Z)\,\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{C}}p
\]

with mass conservation and \(Q^{\ast}1=0\) as the cheap lemmas, and the Hopf remainder as imported spine fact. Name one \(A[p]\) before a run. Report life-drift \(D\) separately from alignment.

That is the difference between a loading construct and a static flux lattice: the lattice is what you get after \(\sigma\) has already won; the construct is the space in which \(\sigma\) can still be swept.

If the analogy is pushed one step without extra physics: a catalog that forgot it was loaded is the lattice after \(\sigma\) won; the construct is the reminder that loading is still possible; the fiber is the part of that reminder the base cannot invert.

That is a Model. It is not a theorem that reality is a Hopf bundle. It is a way to keep the white room from collapsing into another inventory of already-named things.
